Five in the Bed

16 scenes, still images only — nothing animated yet. Check the cast stays the same all the way through, then tell me which scene numbers to redo.

What I need from you: look for any animal that changes colour, pyjamas or species between scenes, any scene where the count looks wrong, and any picture that is just dull. Give me the numbers and I will regenerate only those.

Every frame after the first is an edit of one master frame, which is what keeps the room and the five characters identical. Video comes after you approve — that is the expensive step, so it happens once.

One change I had to make: five in the bed, not ten. I tried ten repeatedly and the model will not hold ten distinct animals in one picture — every attempt came back with twelve or thirteen, with duplicate species. That breaks the counting, which is the whole point of the tap-along. Five renders perfectly every time, every face is big enough to see on a phone, counting to five in Spanish is the right level for a three-year-old, and the story is shorter — which is what you asked for. Say the word if you want me to push on ten anyway.

Who they are, and the order they fall out: penguin, fox, puppy, piglet — leaving the lamb alone at the end. None of them is a bunny, bear, duck or cat on purpose, so your son's own friend never has a twin on screen.

The five ideas land like this: the verse is chanted over a real beat every time they roll (1); scene 3 stops and lets him tap-count (2); the band loses an instrument at every count so the room gets quieter as the bed empties (3); shot sizes alternate wide / medium / close for a real edit rhythm (4); and his own friend is the one who climbs in at scene 14 and is named in every single verse (5).
